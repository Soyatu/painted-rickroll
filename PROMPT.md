# The prompt

This is the single prompt the piece answers: the original prompt, extended with the direction given
while the piece was being made (length, ambition, music).

> **The painted Rickroll**
> Recreate Rick Astley’s unmistakable dance as an HTML animation about one minute long, made from bold,
> moving brushstrokes and synced to music. His coat, microphone, and shadows should swing with exaggerated
> rhythm. Make it a piece of art: artistic, beautiful, polished and incredibly fun. One minute is a guide,
> not a limit; take longer if the piece needs it to end well. Draw everything in code, including the music;
> no external media or website UI.
